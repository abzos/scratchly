Solana rent explained: why every token account locks SOL

Every token account on Solana holds a small deposit of SOL. Here is what that deposit is, where the number comes from, and why it adds up in an active wallet.

Updated November 2026

What rent means on Solana

Solana stores account data on the network, and storage is not free. To keep an account alive, it must hold a minimum balance of SOL. This is called the rent-exempt minimum. Despite the name, you do not pay rent over time. The SOL stays inside the account as a refundable deposit, and you get it back when the account is closed.

Where 0.00203928 SOL comes from

A standard token account stores 165 bytes of data. The network adds 128 bytes of account overhead, and charges 6,960 lamports per byte for two years of storage, which is the rent-exempt rule. The math is:

(165 + 128) x 6,960 = 2,039,280 lamports = 0.00203928 SOL

Token-2022 accounts can carry extensions. Extensions need more space, so those accounts lock slightly more than the standard amount.

Why you end up with so many accounts

A new token account is created whenever your wallet receives a token it has never held: a swap, an airdrop, a memecoin purchase. When you sell everything, the balance goes to zero, but the account stays open and the deposit stays locked. Wallets rarely show this, so most people never notice.

Getting it back

Closing an empty token account returns the full deposit to the wallet you choose. Only accounts with a zero balance can be closed. If you later receive the same token again, a new account is created and a new deposit is paid. Nothing is lost by closing.

You can close accounts one by one with command line tools, or use a cleaner that groups them into a few transactions. Our guide on how to close empty token accounts walks through the options.

Quick answers

Is Solana rent a fee I pay every month?

No. It is a one-time deposit kept inside the account. You get it back when the account is closed.

Do I lose my tokens if I close an account?

No. Only accounts with a zero balance can be closed, so there are no tokens inside to lose.
