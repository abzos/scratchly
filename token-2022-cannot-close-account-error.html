Token-2022 cannot close account: error 35 and withheld fees

You try to close an empty Token-2022 account and the transaction fails with custom program error 35. The cause is almost always withheld transfer fees.

Updated November 2026

The error

When closing a Token-2022 account fails, the simulation or the explorer shows custom program error: 0x23. In decimal that is 35. In the Token-2022 program this error is named AccountHasWithheldTransferFees.

What it means

Some Token-2022 mints use the transfer fee extension. When tokens move, a small fee is withheld inside the receiving token account. An account that still holds withheld fees cannot be closed, even if its visible balance is zero. The program protects those fees from being lost.

How to fix it

The withheld fees must be moved out of the account first. Token-2022 has a permissionless instruction for this, HarvestWithheldTokensToMint. It sends the withheld amount to the mint, where the mint authority can collect it. Anyone can run it, and it does not touch your balance. After the harvest, the close instruction succeeds in the same transaction.

Doing it automatically

A wallet cleaner that supports Token-2022 should detect the withheld amount in the account, add the harvest instruction before the close, and simulate the transaction before asking you to sign. If an account still cannot be closed after that, a good tool skips it and continues with the rest instead of failing the whole batch.

Other reasons a close can fail

  • The account still holds tokens. Burn or move them first.
  • The account is frozen by the mint authority.
  • The signer is not the owner or the close authority.

For the basics, see how to close empty token accounts on Solana.

Quick answers

Is error 35 dangerous?

No. It only means the account cannot be closed yet because it holds withheld transfer fees. Harvesting fixes it.

Does harvesting cost me tokens?

The withheld fees were never yours. Harvesting moves them to the mint and does not affect your balance.
