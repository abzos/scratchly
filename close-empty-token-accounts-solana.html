How to close empty token accounts on Solana and get your SOL back

Closing empty token accounts returns about 0.002 SOL per account. This guide shows how to find them, three ways to close them, and what to check before you sign.

Updated November 2026

Before you start

Only token accounts with a zero balance can be closed. Accounts that still hold tokens, and frozen accounts, cannot be closed. Closing never touches your tokens, because an empty account has none. If you receive the token again later, a new account is created automatically.

Step 1: Find your empty accounts

Open your wallet address in a Solana explorer and look at the token list, or paste it into a wallet check tool. The tool counts the empty accounts and shows how much SOL is locked in them. A free, read-only check does not need a wallet connection and signs nothing.

Step 2: Pick a method

  • Command line. Developers can close accounts with the Solana token tools, one account at a time. It is precise but slow for dozens of accounts.
  • Your wallet. Some wallets let you close individual token accounts. Many do not show empty accounts at all.
  • A cleaner. A wallet cleaner scans your wallet, groups the closes into a few transactions, and shows the total before you sign. This is the fastest route for large wallets.

Step 3: Review what you sign

A legitimate cleaner transaction contains only a few kinds of instructions: closing accounts, optionally burning tokens you selected, and a clearly shown fee. The released SOL must go to your own wallet. If the prompt asks for anything else, reject it. Read more in our guide on whether closing token accounts is safe.

Common problems

  • Some accounts fail to close. Token-2022 accounts that hold withheld transfer fees need a harvest step first. See the Token-2022 close error explained.
  • The numbers look small. Each account returns about 0.002 SOL. The total grows with the number of accounts. See Solana rent explained.

Quick answers

How much SOL do I get per account?

About 0.00204 SOL for a standard token account. Token-2022 accounts with extensions can hold slightly more.

Can I close an account that still has tokens?

No. Burn or move the tokens first. Only empty accounts can be closed.
