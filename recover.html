What each module does

Stake accounts

Lists stake accounts you control that are not delegated or are fully deactivated. Withdrawing returns the whole balance, including your original stake. The fee applies only to the account deposit, never to your stake.

Wrapped SOL

Wrapped SOL is SOL held in a token account, often left over after a swap. Unwrapping closes the account and returns the SOL. The fee applies only to the account deposit.

Lookup tables

For developers. Address lookup tables hold a deposit. Deactivate a table you no longer use, wait a few minutes, then close it to get the deposit back.

Program buffers

For developers. Failed or abandoned deployments can leave buffer accounts that hold SOL. Closing them returns the deposit. Keep a buffer if you plan to upgrade a program soon.
