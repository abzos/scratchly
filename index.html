Questions

Can Scratchly touch my funds?

No. Scratchly never has your keys. Every transaction is built in your browser and signed in your own wallet. You can read exactly what you sign before approving.

What is rent?

Every token account on Solana locks a small deposit of SOL. When an account is empty and closed, that deposit is returned to you.

Will it close accounts that hold tokens?

Never by default. Only empty accounts are selected. Tokens with a balance can be burned and closed only if you pick them yourself in the advanced section.

How do holder fees work?

The app reads your balance of the token when you connect. Holding enough puts you in a lower fee tier automatically. Nothing is staked or locked.
